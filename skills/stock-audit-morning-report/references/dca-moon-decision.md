# DCA + monthly floating-profit decision policy

## Role and authority

This policy records Anthony's approved discussion with Hermes Domina, refined by the later instruction to provide judgement and entry guidance, not quantitative execution. “月相” means profit cohorts by calendar month, not astronomical moon phases. It does not authorize account actions or implement a cash/position ledger.

## Approved capital-flow concept

- External deposits and scheduled DCA contributions are principal, not profit.
- Floating profit is the allocation basis; do not silently replace it with “realized profit only.” Keep economic principal/profit attribution separate from tax cost basis and realized gains.
- Month M floating-profit cohort → M+1 tactical short-term pool → M+2 remaining cohort value to qualified core holdings / Dead Stack, isolated from tactical use.
- Losses reduce the remaining cohort; do not replenish tactical losses from core principal or Dead Stack. Never assign the same profit simultaneously to two pools or repeatedly redistribute an already allocated gain.
- A paper gain is not spendable cash. Under the approved concept, selling an equivalent asset amount supplies cash before tactical use; this is a prerequisite to describe, NOT permission for the skill to sell, borrow, transfer, or place trades. Do not treat a virtual label as cash settlement.
- Example only, ignoring fees/taxes and core-price changes: principal10,000, market value13,000; release3,000 to tactical cash, leaving core market value10,000. A tactical loss200 leaves2,800 for later Dead Stack. Total remains12,800, not15,800. Selling3,000 may recognize only692.31 of tax gain under proportional basis; that does not reduce the economic floating-profit release to692.31. The core can still lose market value.
- Actual records must reconcile deposits, withdrawals, sales, costs, prior cohort allocations and losses. Do not invent a universal monthly-profit formula or assume all positive monthly changes are distributable. If records are absent, give research and conditional pool suitability only; do not claim an available profit amount, cash balance or completed transfer.
- Core / Dead Stack requires a supported business thesis. A failed short-term trade does not become a long-term core merely by relabeling it. Locking holdings does not lock their market value; pre-agreed thesis-break/exit conditions still apply.

## DCA and existing PVA

- Monthly fixed review and baseline budget, not mandatory execution. Unused budget does not automatically become a larger or doubled next-period purchase.
- Reuse the current pinned analyzer/runtime and its reported timeframe/settings. Check its source receipts rather than replacing the formula with a different Domina/Pine version. Do not retune the signal against known outcomes or invent a new strategy parameter set.
- Use PVA descriptively: normalized position, velocity and acceleration show extension, deceleration, strengthening or weakening. Positive acceleration while velocity is negative does not by itself confirm reversal. Describe evidence and limitations, then use Nexus structure/reaction to qualify entry and adds.
- Existing full/reduced/blocked terminology may describe support for the planned review, but do not calculate a size multiplier, target weight, shares, order amount, allocation percentage, reserve or cap. Do not fabricate Sigmoid x0/k or permission thresholds. Unspecified sizing values are not a blocker for qualitative judgement and source-bound price levels.
- Strategy PVA is distinct from panic/alert-gate indicators. Its role is not a standalone buy/sell instruction or automatic core liquidation.
- Same-timeframe closed bars only; lower-timeframe partial bars cannot update a higher-timeframe official value. W1 primary regime must remain anchored to the same completed weekly data; describe D1/internal shifts separately, not as an unexplained change in unchanged W1 history.

## Short / medium / long judgement in the existing report

Do not silently invent fixed holding durations. Use supplied horizons; otherwise express roles:

| Role | Question | Required judgement |
|---|---|---|
| Short | Is a tactical opportunity developing for an eligible prior-month pool? | D1 structure, momentum, numeric zone and confirmation; no assumption of available tactical cash |
| Medium | Is a staged core build justified? | Fundamental/valuation context, structure repair/pullback, numeric add and cancellation conditions |
| Long | Does the company merit core / Dead Stack consideration? | Supported business thesis and deterioration/exit conditions; not simply one-day price direction |

Use the current single-stock disclosure, not separate scattered tables. Retain **基本面分析 → 技術分析 → 操作 → 總結**; operations stay **進場時機／加碼條件／止損／止盈**.

- Entry: source-bound price/zone + the required close/reclaim/retest, with K-line timeframe and as-of date. Touching a zone is not confirmation.
- Add: source-bound confirmation and when the thesis remains intact, not automatic averaging down; no amount or percentage.
- Invalidation: numeric level and required close/reaction when evidenced; distinguish cancellation of a future setup from a proven protective stop for a position already held.
- Exit/target: only source-backed scenario prices and business-thesis conditions. Do not manufacture 2R targets past a nearer opposing barrier or imply guaranteed fills/profits.
- If not actionable now, retain future conditional entry prices and concise interpretation. Missing required market/research evidence follows the existing exclusion gates; missing personal account data does not itself exclude a fully researched stock.
- Preserve the established fonts, Nexus palette, Taiwan positive-red/negative-green convention, compact preview and collapsible detail. No new panels, ledger UI or long strategy exposition. Account-specific DCA/monthly rules need not be repeated when they do not affect the day's judgement.

## No-lookahead and validation

All original production-clock rules remain in force. Evaluate profit eligibility and signal state only from information available by that report's cutoff, not eventual month-end totals or later sales. A previously unavailable balance cannot be financed by a future profit.

For requested walk-forward checks, isolate each daily prefix and lock directional judgements before outcomes. Keep direction, entry confirmation and actionability separate; no-trade is not a directional hit. Short-term hit rate alone does not validate medium/long core accumulation or monthly fund flows. This skill-only change does not implement new backtest/accounting code or authorize a fresh performance claim.
